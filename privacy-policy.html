Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

It accurately describes what the By the Horns app actually collects and does as of this writing, but it has not been reviewed by an attorney. By the Horns ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the By the Horns app collects, how it's used, and your choices. By using By the Horns, you agree to the practices described here.

1. There Is No Account, and No Server Storing Your Data

By the Horns does not require or offer account creation. There is no sign-up, no login, and no email or username collected by us. Your lesson progress, XP, streak, unlocked glossary terms, and hearts are stored only on your device, using standard local app storage. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to this information. If you delete the app or clear its data, this information is gone — we don't have a copy.

2. Advertising

By the Horns shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to unlock an extra heart or earn XP toward unlocking a lesson) through Google's AdMob advertising service. To do this, the app uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which:

  • Collects an advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA on iOS, Google's Advertising ID on Android) used to deliver and measure ads
  • May use Apple's SKAdNetwork to attribute ad performance without directly identifying you
  • On iOS, only collects a tracking-capable identifier if you approve the App Tracking Transparency prompt the app shows you; if you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized using data from other apps or websites

Google acts as an independent controller of the advertising data it collects through its SDK. You can review Google's practices in the Google Privacy Policy and manage ad personalization through your device's system settings (Settings → Privacy → Tracking on iOS; Settings → Ads on Android).

3. In-App Purchases

By the Horns offers an optional one-time "Premium" purchase that removes ads and unlocks additional content. Payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play — we never see or store your payment card details.

To manage your purchase and confirm what you're entitled to (e.g., whether you've already bought Premium), the app uses RevenueCat, a third-party purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives transaction and purchase-identifier information from Apple/Google on our behalf in order to keep your entitlement in sync if you reinstall the app or switch devices. RevenueCat's own privacy practices are described at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. What We Don't Do

  • We don't collect your name, email, or any other account information — there's nothing to collect, since there's no account.
  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't have sponsors or partners that receive your learning activity.
  • We don't operate any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs beyond the advertising and purchase-management services described above.

5. Your Choices

  • Ad tracking: control this anytime in your device's system privacy settings, or by responding to the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Your local data: uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) removes your progress, since it was never stored anywhere else.
  • Purchases: manage or request a refund for a purchase through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings — we can't process refunds directly, since we never receive your payment.

California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA related to the advertising identifier collected through Google's SDK, described above, including the right to opt out of its use for cross-app advertising via your device settings. To ask a question about this policy, contact us at gooliebell@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

By the Horns is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the app shows advertising, if you believe a child under 13 is using the app, please have them stop and contact us at gooliebell@gmail.com so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

Because your learning progress stays on your device rather than passing through a server we operate, the greatest protection is simply your device's own security (passcode, biometric lock, etc.). Data sent to Google (for ads) and RevenueCat (for purchases) is subject to their respective security practices, linked above.

8. International Users

By the Horns is available worldwide. Advertising and purchase-management data described in this policy may be processed by Google and RevenueCat in the United States or other countries where they operate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's data practices change — for example, if a future version adds an account system or a new SDK. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
