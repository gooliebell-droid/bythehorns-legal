Frequently Asked Questions

I uninstalled the app (or got a new phone) and lost all my progress. Can I get it back?

By the Horns doesn't use an account or sign-in — your lesson progress, XP, and streak are stored only on your device, so we have no way to restore them if the app is uninstalled or you switch devices.

Why am I out of hearts / how do hearts work?

Hearts limit how many new lessons you can start in a row — they regenerate over time on their own, or you can watch a short ad for an extra one right away.

I watched an ad but didn't get the reward. What happened?

This can occasionally happen if an ad is closed before it finishes loading fully, or from a temporary network hiccup. Try again in a moment — if it keeps happening, let us know what device and app version you're on so we can look into it.

Does By the Horns give personalized financial advice?

No. By the Horns is general financial education only, not personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. See our Terms of Service for details, and talk to a licensed professional for advice specific to your situation.

What data does the app collect?

Very little — there's no account, and your progress stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for the full, honest breakdown of what the app's ad system does collect.

Found a Bug or Have Feedback?

We'd genuinely like to hear it. Email us at gooliebell@gmail.com with what happened, what device/OS version you're using, and — if it's a visual glitch — a screenshot if you can grab one.
